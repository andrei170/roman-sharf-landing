What is grey market? Is it safe?
Grey market means the watch was not sold by a brand-authorized dealer. It is not stolen. It is not fake. Manufacturers control distribution by authorizing specific dealers. Watches that move outside that authorized chain (from a previous buyer, from international markets, from another dealer) are grey market. Luxury Bazaar authenticates every piece. The watches are real. They are certified pre-owned. They are often available at better prices than authorized dealers because there is no brand-mandated markup floor.
